Sefaria Web Components

Reusable Sefaria reading tools for websites and AI conversations.

Experimental open-source project · not an official Sefaria product

Open with the result, not the architecture: this is a toolkit for developers building reading experiences with Sefaria. It is not a replacement for Sefaria's reader. Transition: first establish the library and the people this work serves.

A living library of Jewish texts

Sefaria brings Jewish texts, translations, commentary, and connections into one free, navigable corpus.

Its website is a destination in its own right. Its open data also powers many other digital Torah experiences.

Point to the source, translation, and commentary. Sefaria supplies the library and its relationships; this project makes those building blocks easier to carry into other interfaces.
