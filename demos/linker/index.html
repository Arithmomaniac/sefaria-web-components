Reading across traditions

An authored article demonstrating automatic citation linking.

The opening image of Genesis 1:1 has traveled through centuries of commentary. It can appear in ordinary prose without special markup, alongside a discussion of Shabbat 2a and the shape of a page.

Inline typography should remain intact when a reference crosses emphasis, as in Genesis 1:2, because the host page owns its markup and event listeners.

A Hebrew example

הקורא עשוי לפגוש גם את בראשית א:ג בתוך פסקה עברית רגילה.

This ordinary host link must remain untouched, as must citations inside tables or superscripts.
